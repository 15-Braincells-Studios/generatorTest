Castle Murder Generator

Every level is built only from what is in the Excel file right now: characters and their tags, all five methods, method clues with Blocks, motives, alibis with Needs, contradictions and red herrings. A solver checks that each level has exactly one answer, and every character gets at least one line.

4 · Worth a look in the tables

Things the generator noticed

None of this was changed in Excel. These are places where a text reads oddly once it is filled in, or where a rule could be sharper.

Alibi-specific contradictions are rareThe generator picks one contradiction at random from all the ones that fit. There are 14 general ones and usually only 1 for the specific alibi, so the specific ones show up in only about 1 in 15 levels where they could appear.
Caring for the sickThis alibi needs someone else to have the alibi Sick first, so it almost never shows up.
Not going where they saidThe text says "he'd", which does not fit the Queen, the Princess, the Head of household or the Librarian. "Different story" uses "they'll", which works for everyone.
Descriptions instead of linesSome alibis are written as a description, not as a line someone says: Sick ("Doctor saying character is sick", "seeing character in Infirmary"), Early to bed and Night watch. The generator shows them as they are written.
5 · Reading rules

How the generator reads your Excel

Where the Excel describes something in words, the generator had to choose how to read it. These are those choices, so you can check they match what you mean.

TagsEvery tag column counts, including Tag 12 (sex). A character's name also works as a tag. "A OR B" means one of the two is enough, "A AND B" means both are needed, and "X, but not Y, Z" or "Any but Y" leaves out Y and Z. The words Suspect, Victim, Witness and Explainer in those lists are already handled by the generator.
Method place"Small room 1–4 (random)" becomes Small room 1, 2, 3 or 4, picked per level.
Murderer and victimThe murderer matches the method's Suspect tags and the victim its Victim tags. A motive must fit between the two, including its condition ("same role tag", "different sex", "Suspect is not the King").
Clue GroupsEach group is rolled against its Show chance. If it hits, one random clue from that group and method goes into the level. Clues with "X is not the Suspect" or "X is not the Victim" in Fixed text are skipped when that rule would be broken.
(excluded) and its limits(excluded) names 1–2 innocent characters who are ruled out. If Fixed text limits it ("can't be the Chef or the Head of household", "can only be the King or the Queen", "can only be someone without Drinks tea"), the generator only picks from that group.
Blocks and NeedsThe characters named in an (excluded) clue get that clue's Blocks for the whole level. A blocked character never gets an alibi or red herring that Needs the same thing. This also applies to partners. Lifting also needs the tag Strong. Someone blocked for Sight or Afternoon is never an unnamed Witness.
Fixed-text effectsThese are coded by hand. Poisoned wine: C3 counts as the full key list, D3 rules out the Princess as victim. Secret meeting: C8 rules out the Head Guard and C9 the Weapons master as murderer, D3 rules out the Weapons master as victim. Heavy book: D3 rules out the Weapons master as victim.
(list), (partial list)(list) names everyone in the method's tag. (partial list) names the real murderer or victim plus 2–4 others. If the speaker is on a list, they say "me".
Unnamed speakers"Witness:", "(Witness)" and "(Explainer)" get a character as speaker. Characters who have nothing to say yet go first. A witness is never the person the line is about.
Characters on alibis"X is busy too" makes X a partner who is covered too. If X is a tag (Tutor), the generator picks someone with that tag. "Partner is busy too" picks the partner from the second word: Any & Any means anyone, Staff & Ruler means a Ruler. "Not with the method X" skips the alibi for that method.
Alibi covers the murderFrom ≤ murder time < To, and the alibi place is not the murder place.
Fake alibiAn alibi the murderer could have that covers the murder. Options where the partner vouches for the murderer are skipped, because the partner knows nothing.
ContradictionOne from the general or alibi-specific list, when its tags and every part of its condition fit. The condition can be split with a semicolon. This includes "nobody else has an alibi in the Study room", "nobody else has the alibi Sick", "not for the alibis …" and "not with the method …".
SolverEveryone starts as a possible murderer and victim. Lists and alibis rule people out, and a contradiction cancels the fake alibi. Exactly one motive must remain where both people are still possible.
